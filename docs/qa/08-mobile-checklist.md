# 08 — Mobile checklist

Run on a real phone (iPhone Safari **and** Android Chrome) or in the browser's device mode at
**390 × 844** (iPhone 14). Use the same accounts as before.

General expectations on every screen:
- No sideways scrolling of the page; nothing cut off at the right edge.
- Tap targets are comfortably large (≥ 44 px); inputs don't zoom the page when focused (iOS).
- Bottom tab bar is visible on app pages; dialogs open above everything (nothing overlaps them).
