Welcome to Breedly! 🎉

We're so glad you're here. Let's get your breeder profile set up so you can start showcasing your amazing pets.

Before you can add pets and create breedings, there are just two quick things to take care of:

1

Complete Your Breeder Profile

Head over to Settings and fill in your breeder details — your breedery name, description, and contact info. This helps pet seekers learn about you and builds trust.

2

Add at Least One Location

In Settings → Locations, create your first breeding location. It doesn't need to be published right away — you just need one on file so we know where your pets are based.

Once you've added a location, the Add Pet button on the Pets page will unlock and you'll be ready to go.
