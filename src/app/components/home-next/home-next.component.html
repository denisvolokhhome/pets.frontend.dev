01 Why Breedly

Families are found by trust, not by price.

Bringing home a new pet is a big decision. That's why every listing shows who you're dealing with before it shows what it costs.

  1. Trust you can see

    Breeder profiles, reviews and verification status sit at the top of every listing, so you never have to dig for them.

  2. Health records on file

    Vaccinations, microchips, vet visits and parent documents travel with each puppy or kitten.

  3. Talk about a specific pet

    Each conversation is about one animal, so both sides always know which puppy or kitten you mean.
