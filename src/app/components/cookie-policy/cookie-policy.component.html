1. Cookies and Browser Storage

Cookies are small text files a website saves on your device. Browser storage (called "local storage" and "session storage") works in a similar way. Both let a site remember things between pages and visits.

Breedly itself does not set cookies. We use browser storage only for the purposes listed below. Our network provider, Cloudflare, may set a few security cookies.

2. What We Store and Why

2.1 Strictly Necessary

These are needed for Breedly to work. Without them you cannot sign in.

  • Sign-in token (local storage): keeps you signed in as you move between pages. It is removed when you log out and stops working when it expires.
  • Cookie notice (local storage): remembers that you have seen our cookie notice, so it is not shown again.
  • Cloudflare security cookies: Cloudflare, which carries traffic to Breedly, may set cookies that tell real visitors apart from automated attacks. See the Cloudflare Cookie Policy.

2.2 Preferences

These make Breedly easier to use. They stay on your device and are never used to track you.

  • Your choices (local storage): for example, the last ZIP code you searched, list or card view, and a collapsed side menu.
  • Where you were (session storage): your search filters and place in a list, so the Back button returns you to the same spot. Cleared when you close the browser tab.

3. What We Do Not Use

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, cross-site tracking, third-party analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics), or social media tracking.

4. Managing Cookies and Storage

You can view and delete cookies and site data in your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-party cookies / Site data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

Deleting Breedly's site data signs you out and resets your preferences.
