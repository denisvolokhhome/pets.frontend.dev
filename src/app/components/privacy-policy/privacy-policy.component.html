Privacy at a Glance

  • Breedly is free. We do not ask for or store payment details. If we ever introduce paid plans, we will tell you at least 60 days in advance (see Section 3.1).
  • We collect only what we need to run Breedly: your email address, the profile details you choose to add, your listings, and your messages. Everything we collect is listed in Section 3.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal information, such as Social Security numbers, government ID, payment card or bank details (see Section 3.4).
  • We never sell your data and never share it with advertisers.
  • Your files stay yours. You can view and download the photos and documents you upload at any time, and breeders can download all their data in one file (see Section 12).
  • You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing privacy@breedly.us.

1. Introduction

Breedly ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Breedly platform at breedly.us, a pet breeding management service that connects responsible breeders with pet seekers. Breedly is based in the State of Maryland, United States. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

By using Breedly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. Contact us with privacy questions at privacy@breedly.us or call (240) 242-9483.

2. Who This Policy Covers

Breedly serves three types of users:

  • Breeders: users who manage pets, litters, and individual offspring listings.
  • Pet Seekers: users who browse, favorite, and contact breeders about available offspring.
  • Guests: visitors who browse listings without an account. Guests who send a message are automatically registered as Pet Seeker accounts.

3. Information We Collect

We keep the information we collect to the minimum needed to run Breedly.

3.1 Information You Provide

Account:

  • Email address (required, used to sign in and to contact you)
  • Password, stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text. If you sign up with Google, you do not need a password; you can set one later in Settings.
  • Name (optional)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Account type: Breeder or Pet Seeker

Breeder profile:

  • Breedery name, description, and profile image
  • Search tags, social media links, and website address

Breeder locations:

  • Address (street, city, state, country, ZIP code) and a location name
  • Map coordinates calculated from that address
  • An optional location phone number. It is shown to visitors only if you turn on "Show phone number to pet seekers" for that location.

Pets, litters, and offspring (breeders):

  • Pet names, dates of birth, sex, weight, breed, and description
  • Animal health records: microchip numbers, vaccination status, health certificates, deworming records, and birth certificates
  • Offspring details: name, sex, date of birth, price, availability status, color and markings, and description
  • Photos, and documents such as PDFs of health certificates or pedigrees, that you upload for pets and offspring
  • Breeding planner records, which are visible to you only and never shown to pet seekers: mating dates and methods, sire, progesterone test results, ovulation date, pregnancy stage, and milestone dates

Waitlists and deposits (only if you use the waitlist feature):

  • Waitlist requests (pet seekers): the sex preference, notes, and answers to the breeder's application form that you choose to submit. The breeder you apply to can see this information, together with your name and contact details.
  • Contact details of people who are not Breedly users (breeders only): if a breeder adds someone to their waitlist manually, the breeder enters that person's name, email address, and phone number. We store these details so the breeder can manage their waitlist and contact that person. If that person later signs up or logs in to Breedly with the same email address, their waitlist entry may be linked to their account.
  • Deposit records: if a breeder records a deposit for your waitlist entry (amount, date received, and payment method such as Zelle, Venmo, or cash), the breeder and you can see it. Breedly stores this record but does not process, hold, or verify payments.
  • Waitlist order and matching: the breeder's pick order and which offspring a person is matched to.

Breeders who add other people's contact details are responsible for having permission to do so. If you believe a breeder has added your details to a waitlist and you would like them removed, contact us at privacy@breedly.us.

Messages and reviews:

  • Messages you exchange with other users, and documents or locations a breeder chooses to share in a conversation
  • Reviews pet seekers leave for breeders: rating, tags, and an optional comment

Payments:

Breedly is currently free, and we do not collect payment information. If we introduce paid plans in the future, payments will be handled by our payment processor, Stripe. We would never see or store your full card number. We would store only the identifiers Stripe gives us (encrypted), your plan, and invoice records. This section will be updated before any paid plan starts.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address, used to protect sign-in, registration, and messaging from abuse (see Section 10)
  • Basic technical information your browser sends, such as browser type and device type, which our network provider processes to deliver and protect the site
  • Your device's location, only if you allow it in your browser. The map uses it to show breeders and offspring near you. It is sent with your search and is not saved to your account.
  • Data stored in your browser to keep you signed in and remember your choices (see Section 9)

3.3 Information from Google Sign-In

If you create an account or sign in with your Google account, Google shares with us:

  • Your name, email address, and Google account identifier
  • Confirmation from Google that your email address is verified

We do not receive your Google password. Google's processing of your information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

3.4 What We Do Not Collect

  • Social Security numbers, driver's license, passport, or other government ID numbers
  • Payment card numbers, bank account numbers, or other financial account details
  • Biometric data, or health information about people
  • Advertising identifiers, or tracking of your activity on other websites

Please do not put sensitive personal information about yourself or others into listings, documents, or messages.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to run and protect Breedly:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Show breeder profiles, published locations, and listings to pet seekers
  • Let breeders and pet seekers message each other
  • Show breeders and offspring near a location on the map
  • Send in-app notifications: new messages, favorites, breeding milestone reminders, and, if you use the waitlist feature, waitlist updates
  • Protect accounts and the platform from fraud, spam, and abuse
  • Fix problems and improve features
  • Comply with the law and respond to lawful requests

Emails:

We send transactional emails through an email delivery provider:

  • Account verification and password reset emails
  • A welcome email when you create an account
  • Activity notifications: when you receive a new message and, for breeders, when a pet seeker adds one of your offspring to Favorites or a breeding milestone you're tracking is coming up
  • If you use the waitlist feature, updates about your waitlist requests and entries
  • Important notices about Breedly, such as changes to these policies or to pricing

You can turn activity notifications off in your account settings. Account verification, password reset, security emails, and important notices are needed to operate your account and cannot be turned off. We do not send marketing emails.

Location privacy:

On public maps, breeder locations are shown 0.5 to 1.5 miles away from the real address, so your exact address stays private while pet seekers can still find breeders nearby.

5. Why We Are Allowed to Use Your Information

Breedly is a US-based service, operated under US law from the State of Maryland, and is not directed at users outside the United States. We use your information:

  • To provide the service you signed up for: your account, listings, messages, and notifications.
  • For legitimate business interests: security, fraud prevention, and improving Breedly, where these interests do not override your privacy rights.
  • To meet legal obligations: complying with applicable law, responding to lawful requests, and enforcing our Terms of Use.
  • With your consent, where the law requires it, for example for your device's location. You can withdraw consent at any time.

6. Who Can See Your Information

6.1 Other Users and the Public

  • Breeder profiles, published locations (at an approximate position on the map), and published offspring listings, including their photos, are public.
  • Your microchip numbers, health records, breeding planner records, unpublished locations, and documents are never shown on public pages. Documents are shared only when you choose to share them in a conversation.
  • Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation.
  • Reviews are public and show the reviewer's name, rating, tags, and comment.

6.2 Service Providers

We share information only with the service providers we need to run Breedly:

  • Google (sign-in): if you sign in with Google. Google Privacy Policy
  • Email delivery provider: receives your email address and the content of our emails to you, in order to deliver them.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap (maps and address lookup): receives addresses to calculate map coordinates. OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare (network and security): carries traffic to our servers and protects the site from attacks, which means it processes your IP address and basic browser information. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Hosting: our servers and data storage are located in the United States.
  • Stripe (payments): only if we introduce paid plans and you choose one. Stripe Privacy Policy

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers.

7. Where Your Data Is Stored

Breedly is operated from the United States and is intended for use within the United States. Your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you use Breedly from another country, your information will be transferred to the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

8. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Your account and content: kept while your account is open. Pets, offspring, and messages you remove are hidden from everyone but may remain in our database until your account is deleted.
  • When you ask us to delete your account: we delete your profile, locations, listings, photos, documents, and the messages you sent within 30 days of your request. We may keep a limited record where the law requires it, for example to deal with a legal claim.
  • Waitlist and deposit records: Retained until the breeder deletes their account. Anyone listed on a breeder's waitlist can ask the breeder, or contact privacy@breedly.us, to have their details removed.
  • Security data: IP addresses used to protect sign-in, registration, and messaging are kept only briefly.
  • Address lookup cache: address-to-coordinate lookups are cached for up to 24 hours.
  • Data exports: an export you create under Settings → Export data is stored privately and deleted 24 hours after it is ready, or sooner if you create a new one.

9. Cookies and Browser Storage

Breedly does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We store a small amount of data in your browser to keep you signed in, remember that you've seen our cookie notice, and remember choices such as search filters. Our network provider, Cloudflare, may also set cookies that are strictly necessary to protect the site. See our Cookie Policy for details.

10. Security

We protect your data with:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data sent between your device and Breedly
  • Password hashing, so passwords are never stored in plain text
  • Access controls, so each user can see and change only their own data
  • Rate limits on sign-in, registration, and messaging, which use your IP address, to block password-guessing and spam

No system is 100% secure. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

11. Children's Privacy

You must be at least 18 years old to use Breedly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at privacy@breedly.us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Your Choices and Rights

12.1 Download Your Files and Data

  • You can view and download the photos and documents you upload at any time, from the pet or offspring they belong to.
  • Breeders can download all their data at any time under Settings → Export data: one ZIP file with spreadsheets of their locations, pets, breedings, and offspring, including sold ones, plus all their photos and documents.

12.2 Access, Correction, and Deletion

You can see and edit most of your information yourself in Breedly. You can also ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete your account and personal information
  • Limit how we use your information, or object to a particular use
  • Withdraw a consent you have given

To make a request, email privacy@breedly.us from the email address on your account, or call (240) 242-9483. We will respond within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

California and other state residents: depending on where you live, state law may give you the right to know, access, correct, and delete your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our response, and you may contact your state's Attorney General.

13. Third-Party Links

Breedly may link to other websites, such as a breeder's social media profiles. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.
